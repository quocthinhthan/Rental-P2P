# RentHub product context

Web marketplace baseline for the design handoff · 23 November 2026

## Product and audience

RentHub is a Vietnamese peer-to-peer marketplace for short-term rentals of personal items such as cameras, camping gear and tools. Renters can find an item near them for a date range, see the rental fee and deposit, and track the transaction through return. Owners can earn from items they already own, manage availability, assess requests and document the condition of their items. Identity verification, electronic contracts, photo-backed handover and return, and a dispute process address the trust gap between people who may be meeting for the first time.

The audience descriptions below are based on the product and its workflows. The repository establishes no validated demographic segments, launch geography, or device mix. The Founder uses **RentHub** in this handoff. The current site also uses **RentalP2P**, while the requested filenames use `RENTALHUB`. Production brand spelling awaits confirmation. ## Personas and permissions

| Persona | Primary goal | Verified access and gates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest | Find a suitable item and decide whether to rent | Browse, search and filter, inspect availability, view public profiles and reviews, read help and register or sign in. Sign-in is required to save a favorite, file an item report or create a rental. |
| Member | Manage access, profile and readiness to transact | Update profile and bank details, submit eKYC, manage favorites and see personal rental and financial views. Restrictions apply to suspended and banned accounts. |
| Verified member | Become eligible for identity-gated transaction steps | eKYC is required by the backend to create a rental request or listing, sign a rental contract and record pickup. Owner confirmation requires both parties to be verified. The web also prompts for bank name, account number and account holder before booking or posting/editing a listing. |
| Renter | Book, coordinate pickup and return, and resolve problems | Contextual role for a rental. Pays, signs, uses rental chat, can record or approve eligible handovers, and can report a problem or review the counterparty when eligible. Can cancel before pickup proof in the allowed stages. |
| Owner | List items, assess bookings and protect item condition | Contextual role for a listing or rental. Edits/deletes owned listings within server rules, manages blocked dates, accepts or rejects a paid request, signs, records or approves handovers, and can use chat, disputes and reviews. |
| Admin | Monitor marketplace health and moderate cases | Admin APIs gate dashboards, user and listing moderation, audit history, item reports and dispute resolution. Can read rental chat for review. The shared web route wrapper checks sign-in and admin navigation is role-gated. Admin API authorization remains the boundary for admin data and mutations. |

One member can rent and lend under the same account. Owner and renter actions depend on the item or transaction, current status, and which participant submitted the record. ## Verified end-to-end journeys

1. **Discover and compare.** Home shows featured and popular listings with keyword entry points. Shop supports keyword, category, address text, desired dates, featured-only and map/radius search. Public owner links can scope results. A renter evaluates photo, daily price, pickup location, available dates, listing description, item-specific reviews and owner trust signals. Members can save and remove favorites, up to 100. [Shop](../../frontend/src/pages/ShopPage.jsx) · [Item detail](../../frontend/src/pages/ItemDetailPage.jsx) · [Favorites rules](../../backend/controllers/favorites.controller.js)
2. **Prepare and list.** A member verifies identity and completes bank details as applicable. A new listing takes a name, formatted description, category or new category, positive daily price and item value, deposit percentage, photos and mapped pickup/return location. The web allows up to five listing images and supports a description preview and optional price suggestion. Owner detail provides an upcoming schedule with add/remove date blocks. Owner editing and deletion are available from rental management. Date blocks validate their range and check overlapping bookings and other blocks. [Post and edit listing](../../frontend/src/pages/PostItemPage.jsx) · [Item rules](../../backend/controllers/items.controller.js) · [Owner inventory](../../frontend/src/pages/MyRentalsPage.jsx)
3. **Request and pay.** An eligible renter chooses a start/end date and can add a note. Their own or delisted items cannot be booked. The request is created before the external VNPay redirect. Item detail estimates the rental fee from the inclusive duration, shows the deposit based on item value and deposit percentage, and adds both to show the estimated total. The VNPay confirmation uses the amount calculated by the backend when it creates the rental. Commission is 10% of the rental fee and the calculated owner amount is the remaining rental fee. Verified success advances to owner confirmation. Payment failure permits retry on an unpaid order. A lost availability race can produce cancellation with a recorded refund status. [Booking and payment rules](../../backend/controllers/rentals.controller.js) · [VNPay result](../../frontend/src/pages/VNPayReturnPage.jsx)
4. **Accept and sign.** The owner sees the request and renter trust summary, then accepts or rejects it. Acceptance rechecks availability and both identities and creates an electronic contract. Each participant signs separately using the signature pad. The initial signed PDF is queued for email to both parties once both have signed, subject to worker and email delivery. The contract is viewable in rental detail. [Rental detail](../../frontend/src/pages/RentalDetailPage.jsx) · [Contract and payment handlers](../../backend/controllers/rentals.controller.js) · [Email worker](../../notification-worker/worker.js)
5. **Coordinate pickup and record condition.** Rental detail has a transaction-specific text chat with history, real-time delivery and a REST sending fallback. Both signatures are required before pickup. One party records a photo-backed report. The other party can review, edit allowed fields or photos, and confirm it. The report recorder sees a waiting state and cannot approve their own report. Reports hold condition, accessories, notes and evidence. [Rental chat](../../frontend/src/components/Rentals/RentalChatPanel.jsx) · [Handover form](../../frontend/src/components/Rentals/HandoverModal.jsx) · [Rental handlers](../../backend/controllers/rentals.controller.js)
6. **Return and follow up.** During the rental either participant can record a return report with photos, condition, accessories, notes and damage details as applicable. The other confirms before the rental becomes complete. The final PDF with the pickup/return annex is queued for email. Participants can submit one review each on a completed rental within seven days of its recorded update time. The renter can use **Thuê lại sản phẩm** after cancellation, rejection or completion to reopen the listing and start a new booking. Each member can view computed transaction summaries and history for both roles. [Rental detail](../../frontend/src/pages/RentalDetailPage.jsx) · [Review rules](../../backend/controllers/reviews.controller.js) · [Financial view](../../backend/controllers/views.controller.js)
7. **Handle exceptions.** In web rental detail the renter can cancel when awaiting payment, awaiting owner confirmation or confirmed, before pickup proof. Owner rejection is available while awaiting confirmation and owner cancellation is exposed on confirmed orders before pickup proof. The cancellation API also permits an owner to cancel a pending-confirmation order. Once pickup proof exists, cancellation is blocked. Depending on status, participants can report a rental incident and enter mediation. A listing violation has its own reporting and moderation process. [Rental actions](../../frontend/src/pages/RentalDetailPage.jsx) · [Rental cancellation](../../backend/controllers/rentals.controller.js) · [Dispute rules](../../backend/controllers/disputes.controller.js)

## Rental state and next actor

| Rental state | Status label in the web | What moves it forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pending_payment` | Chờ thanh toán | Renter completes VNPay payment. An unpaid request does not reserve the dates. |
| `pending_confirmation` | Chờ xác nhận | Owner accepts or rejects. A paid request is treated as an overlapping booking. |
| `confirmed` | Đã xác nhận | Both verified parties sign. Either eligible participant records pickup, then the counterpart approves it. The order remains confirmed while waiting for signatures or pickup approval. |
| `in_progress` | Đang thuê | Either participant records return. The counterpart approves it. The order stays in progress while return approval is pending. |
| `completed` | Hoàn tất | Normal completion follows return approval. Dispute resolution in favor of the owner can also set this state. Reviews and renter rebooking are available according to eligibility. |
| `cancelled` / `rejected` | Đã hủy / Đã từ chối | Stop the ordinary progression. Renter may reopen the item to book again if available. Show the payment outcome separately. |
| `disputed` | Đang tranh chấp | Applicable transaction actions are frozen. Mediation, eligible withdrawal/escalation and admin resolution govern the next step. |

The timeline labels are **Thanh toán → Chờ xác nhận → Ký hợp đồng → Giao/Nhận đồ → Đang thuê → Hoàn tất**. Within a step the user needs to know whether they can act or are waiting for payment, owner acceptance, the other signature, or the other party’s approval. The domain guide maps an active dispute to a warning on **Đang thuê**. That display convention can coexist with a dispute opened before pickup, so the case view must communicate the actual evidence and approvals. Restored or resolved cases must reflect the outcome and underlying rental state. Payment is tracked independently as `pending`, `escrowed` or `refunded`. ## Trust, safety and moderation

| Surface | Implemented behavior and design implication |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | A member starts Viettel AI verification from **Tài khoản** using a QR or direct session. The flow checks both sides of the CCCD and completes five liveness poses. The backend saves the result and keeps the CCCD linked to the account if re-verification is required. Show verification and expiry states in context. [Account](../../frontend/src/pages/AccountPage.jsx) · [Identity flow](../EKYC_VIETTEL_AI.md) |
| Public and counterparty signals | Public profile shows safe identity summary, verification, trust level, reliability score, review stars/count and public listings. The owner and counterparty summaries support the rental decision. TrustScore is 0–100 and starts at 50 for a new user. Public review rating is a separate 1–5 star measure. Item-detail stars are based on public reviews about the owner tied to rentals of that item. Keep unrated distinct from poorly rated. [Public profiles](../../backend/controllers/users.controller.js) · [TrustScore](../../backend/services/trustScore.service.js) · [Item reviews](../../backend/controllers/reviews.controller.js) |
| Review privacy | The first review stays hidden until both submit or the review ages seven days and an eligible review read publishes it. Review publication after the wait is triggered by user/item review reads. The public profile reads reviews already public. Make submitted, still hidden and publicly visible states understandable. [Review handlers](../../backend/controllers/reviews.controller.js) · [Public profile handler](../../backend/controllers/users.controller.js) |
| Evidence and verification of handover | Pickup and return each require at least one image on submission. Recording, waiting for the other party, counterpart review/edit and confirmation are distinct. Capture optional issue details in the appropriate report. Preserve the view and print options for the contract and the separately viewable pickup and return records. [Handover form](../../frontend/src/components/Rentals/HandoverModal.jsx) · [Contract viewer](../../frontend/src/components/Rentals/ContractModal.jsx) · [Rental detail](../../frontend/src/pages/RentalDetailPage.jsx) |
| Rental incident | **Báo cáo sự cố** opens an order-specific complaint. Web form requires a reason category and 10–500 characters of detail and accepts up to three optional image attachments of up to 5 MB each. A complaint freezes the rental and opens 48 hours of **Đang hòa giải**. Either party can request admin intervention after the deadline. The reporter can currently withdraw while pending or escalated and restore the previous state. Chat remains available to the parties. [Incident form](../../frontend/src/components/Rentals/DisputeModal.jsx) · [Dispute handlers](../../backend/controllers/disputes.controller.js) · [Rental chat](../../frontend/src/pages/RentalDetailPage.jsx) |
| Dispute decision | Admin can review pending or escalated cases with parties, reported reason, evidence, rental context and read-only chat. Resolution records the decision and winner. Renter win cancels and can mark captured payment for refund. Owner win completes the order. No winner restores previous rental/item state and currently requires no penalty. Where allowed, optional penalties include warning, seven-day suspension or ban for a related participant. Show dispute status and result separately from rental and payment status. [Dispute detail](../../frontend/src/pages/AdminDisputeDetailPage.jsx) · [Resolution rules](../../backend/controllers/disputes.controller.js) |
| Listing violation | **Báo cáo vi phạm** is a separate item-level report. Signed-in members can report an item once. The form collects a category, 10–500 characters and up to three optional images of up to 5 MB each. Administrative options include dismissal, warning and actions against the listing. Later availability changes preserve the original resolution and penalty history. [Listing report form](../../frontend/src/components/Items/ReportItemModal.jsx) · [Item report handler](../../backend/controllers/items.controller.js) · [Admin moderation](../../backend/controllers/admin.controller.js) |
| Private data | Scope contract and handover details to authorized transaction views, verification and bank information to account settings, and sensitive investigation material to approved admin contexts. Use fictional listing, participant, contract and evidence content in all boards. Contact disclosure on item detail is an open decision. [Safe trust summary rules](../../.agents/skills/trust-score-rules/SKILL.md) · [Chat authorization](../../backend/services/chatAuthorization.service.js) |
